PRIVACY

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Grid Proto is a free game prototype with no accounts or sign-ups. We never ask for your name, email address or any other personal detail.

Your save data stays on your device. Campaign progress is saved in your browser's local storage and is never sent to us. Clearing this site's data deletes it.

Who receives data. The game is hosted by Vercel, which processes requests (including IP addresses) to deliver it. The pixel font is loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser fetches it. There are no analytics, ads, cookies or tracking, and nothing is sold.
